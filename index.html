Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you install.

How do I loop a part of a YouTube video?

Install Repeatly, open any YouTube video, click the repeat button next to the volume control, then set a start and end time by dragging the timeline or typing exact timestamps. Hit Start Loop and that section repeats automatically.

Can I change YouTube playback speed with Repeatly?

Yes. Repeatly adds one-click speed presets from 0.5× to 2× directly in its panel, so you don't need to dig through YouTube's settings menu.

Can I save a YouTube loop to come back to later?

Yes. Tap the save button on any loop to add it to your personal "My Loops" library. You can reopen a saved loop anytime, even if it's on a different video, and Repeatly will jump straight to that moment.

Is Repeatly free?

Yes, Repeatly is completely free with no sign-up, no account, and no ads.
